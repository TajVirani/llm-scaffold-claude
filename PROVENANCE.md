## Vendored

**Policy (D71, D77, D78):** vendored skills are temporary references for building our first-party versions, removed once rewritten. We ship a first-party version of anything that affects how agents work; once it exists (or the skill is dropped) and its lineage is recorded in `upstream-lineage.json`, the vendored skill is deleted. Add-ons unrelated to how agents work (the anti-slop Oxlint plugins) are not rebuilt; they are installed from upstream at a pinned commit (`kind: pinned-addon`). Vendored skills are never installed into target projects: `install.sh` copies only `.agents/install-manifest.txt`, which names no vendored skill. Do not build first-party skills that depend on them at runtime; cite upstream (`<repo>/<path>@<commit>`) in `Sources:` lines, not a vendored path.

No vendored skills remain (D78): `handoff` and `writing-for-agents` are first-party rewrites (rows under Workflow 2.0). `skills-lock.json`, which pinned their upstream hashes, was deleted by D79; to vendor a skill again, record its upstream commit in `upstream-lineage.json`.

Deleted under D78, with their lock entries and `vendored_at` paths (lineage of what we took stays in `upstream-lineage.json`): from mattpocock/skills `ask-matt`, `code-review`, `codebase-design`, `diagnosing-bugs`, `grill-me`, `grill-with-docs`, `grilling`, `implement`, `implement-spec`, `improve-codebase-architecture`, `pr`, `prototype`, `research`, `retro`, `setup-matt-pocock-skills`, `tdd`, `to-questionnaire`, `to-spec`, `to-tickets`, `triage`, `wait-what`, `wayfinder`, `wizard`, `zoom-out`; from dmmulroy/anti-slop `install-anti-slop` (the rules are still fetched at setup, D42); from athola/claude-night-market `proof-of-work`, `clear-context`. ## Copied from vendors (D74)

Vendored files that a first-party asset reads at runtime are copied into that asset's own folder and ship with it, so the vendored original can be deleted (D78 deleted `codebase-design`). Each copy has a `Copied from … @ <commit> (<license>). Local edits: …` header and a vendor LICENSE beside it. Each copy has a `kind: copy` entry in `upstream-lineage.json` (repo root, scaffold-only; D77) with the upstream path, commit and sha256, so an upstream pull can be diffed and each change accepted or rejected (procedure in the `_about` field of `upstream-lineage.json`: `scripts/upstream-diff.sh`, then update the entry). Copies made before D74 (`domain-modeling/assets/{GLOSSARY,ADR}-FORMAT.md`, the `review-standards` smell baseline) are adaptations recorded in their rows above, not tracked here.

## Upstream lineage (D77)

Per-asset lineage lives in `upstream-lineage.json`, not in this file: one entry for every first-party asset derived from upstream (skills, modules, references, agents, hooks, scripts, templates), with `kind` `copy`, `derived` or `pinned-addon`, the upstream repo, path and pinned commit, `changes`, `why` and `reviewed_at`. The rows above say what an asset is; the lineage entry says where it came from and what we changed.

## How to maintain

- Add or vendor anything under `.agents/`: add a row here in the same change. A vendored skill also gets its upstream repo and commit recorded in `upstream-lineage.json`.
- Add a first-party asset that targets need: add its line to `.agents/install-manifest.txt` in the same change.
- Local edits to a copied vendor file: record them in the Local edits column of Copied from vendors and in its `upstream-lineage.json` entry, so upgrades preserve them.
- A first-party asset needs a vendored file at runtime: copy it (D74), add a Copied from vendors row and a `kind: copy` entry in `upstream-lineage.json` in the same change.
- Derive or rewrite a first-party asset from upstream, or install an upstream add-on at a pin: add its `upstream-lineage.json` entry (`derived` or `pinned-addon`) in the same change (D77).
- Delete a vendored skill only once its first-party version exists (or it is dropped) and the lineage is recorded (D77); remove its row here.
- Implementing a stub: flip its status to implemented.
- Do not put content in `.claude/`; it only symlinks to `.agents/`.
